About Art Of Stone

Built on craft.
Driven by proportion.

We approach masonry as part of the architecture—not as decoration applied at the end.

Our philosophy

Good stonework should make the building feel more resolved.

The best installation does more than cover a surface. It reinforces the building’s geometry, grounds it visually and creates texture that changes naturally with light.

That means planning corners, window returns, transitions, caps, sill lines and joint rhythm before the first stone is set. We coordinate the small decisions because those are what make the finished façade feel intentional.

Material intelligence

Stone selected for the building, not the trend.

Every material has its own scale, weight, texture and way of catching shadow. We help match stone format and colour to the architecture so the façade remains balanced instead of visually busy.

  • Natural limestone, granite and fieldstone applications
  • Manufactured and thin stone veneer systems
  • Dry-stack and mortared joint aesthetics
  • Architectural caps, sills, columns and entry details

Our standards

The finish is only as good as what sits behind it.

01

Preparation

Substrates, drainage strategy, flashing and transitions are reviewed before finish work begins.

02

Layout

Courses and stone distribution are planned to avoid awkward cuts, repetitive patterns and weak corners.

03

Execution

Alignment, mortar depth, joint profile and cleanup are controlled consistently across the elevation.

04

Inspection

Final details are reviewed from both close range and street view so the composition reads correctly.
