Before you contact us

Useful things to send.

Do I need architectural drawings?

No. Photos and approximate dimensions are enough to start a conversation. Drawings help when the project is new construction or has complex interfaces.

Can I send inspiration photos?

Yes. Inspiration images help us understand stone colour, scale, joint style and the overall architectural direction you prefer.
